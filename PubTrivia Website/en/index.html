Questions.
Honest answers.

Do I need a quiz master?

No. Questions, answers, scores and leaderboard run fully automatically. You don't need to touch a microphone.

What does it cost after the pilot?

After three months free, you decide whether to continue. Fixed monthly fee, comparable to a streaming service — no extra cut of your revenue.

What equipment do I need?

A TV with an HDMI port and internet. We'll help you set it up.

What if no one plays?

Even with no players, the TV shows questions, fun facts and your offers. Often one table starts — then the rest follow.

Can I use it only on quiet nights?

Yes. You choose the schedule. Or let it run 24/7 — both work.

How much does a digital pub quiz cost?

PubTrivia runs free for three months during the pilot. After that you pay a fixed monthly subscription, comparable to a streaming service — no fee on your revenue, no commitments.

Which pub quiz software works in the Netherlands?

PubTrivia is built specifically for Dutch hospitality: it runs in Dutch and English, with Dutch-relevant questions, aimed at cafés, kroegen, and eetcafés. International buzzer systems usually don't fit the NL market.

Does PubTrivia work on a Smart TV without Chromecast?

Yes. Any device with a browser works: Smart-TV, Chromecast, Apple TV, Fire TV, or a mini-PC behind the TV. Open our link in the browser and the quiz runs. No app, no separate hardware.
